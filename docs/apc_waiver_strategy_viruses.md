# APC Waiver Strategy for Viruses

Audit date: 2026-05-01

Target journal: *Viruses* (MDPI)

## Official Policy Points Checked

- MDPI's APC page lists *Viruses* with an article processing charge of CHF
  2600 at the time of checking.
- MDPI states that waiver and discount requests should be made before
  submission and are assessed by the Managing Editor based on paper quality and
  the authors' ability to pay.

Official sources:

- https://www.mdpi.com/apc
- https://www.mdpi.com/journal/viruses/apc

## Recommended Action

Send the waiver email before or at the beginning of submission, attaching:

- `submission_viruses/APC_full_waiver_request_letter.docx`
- student-status proof if available
- brief funding-status proof if requested by the Editorial Office

## Wording Strategy

Use a professional and factual tone:

- Student author.
- Independent public-data computational study.
- No supervising principal investigator.
- No grant, laboratory funding, or institutional APC support.
- Request full APC waiver because the APC is beyond personal capacity.
- State that the waiver request is independent of peer review.

Avoid:

- Emotional or apologetic language.
- Promising acceptance in exchange for payment relief.
- Overstating hardship beyond what can be documented.
