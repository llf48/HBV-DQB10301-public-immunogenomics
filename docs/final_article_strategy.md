# Final Article Strategy

Date: 2026-05-01

## Final Title

Public HBV genotype B/C core diversity reveals an HLA-DQB1*03:01-associated
class-II presentation gap

## Final Claim

The strongest defensible claim is:

**Public HBV genotype B/C core diversity preserves a marked predicted
class-II binding gap for DQB1*03:01-containing HLA-DQ heterodimers, especially
within HBV core/capsid N-terminal windows.**

This is a computational immunogenomics article, not an experimental mechanism
paper.

## Why This Is More Publishable Than The Original Mutation-Loss Idea

The initial idea, HCC-associated HBV coding mutations causing
DQB1*03:01-specific binder-to-non-binder loss, was mechanistically attractive
but did not survive pilot analysis. HBx and PreS/S hotspot pilots were not
strong enough as a main story.

The final story is stronger because it is:

- directly connected to the published DQB1*03:01/HBV-HCC nucleocapsid finding;
- supported by a full public genotype B/C GenBank sequence expansion;
- robust to de-redundancy, bootstrap, method sensitivity, and stratified checks;
- anchored to known IEDB human HBV core/nucleocapsid MHC-II epitopes;
- honest about not having host-virus pairing or wet-lab validation.

## Final Dataset

- Public complete-genome HBV genotype B/C candidate records: 3784.
- QC-passed genotype-labeled core records: 1576.
- Unique QC-passed accessions: 1538.
- Unique HBV core 15-mer windows: 11176.
- Occurrence-weighted HBV core 15-mer windows: 259916.

## Key Result

- DQA1*03:01/DQB1*03:02: 9.55% unique binders, 10.52% occurrence-weighted.
- DQA1*05:08/DQB1*03:01: 1.10% unique binders, 0.23% occurrence-weighted.
- DQA1*06:01/DQB1*03:01: 1.26% unique binders, 0.80% occurrence-weighted.

Occurrence-weighted Fisher comparisons versus DQA1*03:01/DQB1*03:02:

- Versus DQA1*05:08/DQB1*03:01: OR 52.02, P < 1e-300.
- Versus DQA1*06:01/DQB1*03:01: OR 14.50, P < 1e-300.

Less suitable without experiments:

## Reviewer Risk To Preempt

The abstract, discussion, and cover letter must keep saying that this is a
binding-prediction and public-data hypothesis-generating study. Do not imply
actual antigen processing, HLA-DQ surface presentation, CD4 T-cell activation,
or HCC causality.
